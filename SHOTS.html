03 / PHOTOGRAPHY

THROUGH MY LENS

Ordinary places can look different when you stop and see them from another perspective.

Photography allows me to see ordinary places from a different perspective. Through my shots, I capture the beauty of places, buildings, landscapes, skies, and small details that catch my attention. Each photograph represents a moment when something around me was interesting enough to stop and capture.

These are not just photographs of places—they are moments that I chose to remember.
